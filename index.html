Personal records

Your standout runs and memorable picks. Select a date to open the exact predictions in History.

Records use completed predictions only, across every saved season. Dates and times are scheduled game starts in Eastern Time. Runs follow start time; games starting together use game ID order. Gaps between games do not break prediction streaks. Ties keep the earliest record.

Records are calculated from your saved history, including after JSON import. Unpicked games never count.
